## What this is

An executable checklist **Skill** that walks an AI short-drama / animated-drama (漫剧） creator from "production done" to "live on every platform and earning": qualifications audit, platform selection, rights-center account roles, contract models, rights paperwork for AI-generated work, dual-version delivery specs, upload flow, review-rejection troubleshooting, legitimate free-IP channels, monetization & settlement, second-round licensing, overseas and big-screen distribution.

It distills Magic Cat AIGC Studio's hands-on production research and a clause-by-clause review of the platforms' public rule documents. Hand `SKILL.md` to any skill-capable AI agent (Claude Code, Codex, etc.) — or paste it to any AI assistant as an operating procedure — and it will walk you section by section, with a pass/fail criterion for every item.

## Who it's for

- Independent creators and small teams making AI short dramas / AI animated dramas / vertical short dramas
- Anyone whose drama is *made* but stuck on "where do I publish it, under what terms, and how do I get paid"
- Teams without IP, looking for legitimate free-IP adaptation channels (Tomato Novel, Yuewen, Migu, WeChat official-account novels, and more)

## What's inside

`SKILL.md` — one file, 14 sections, every item with a pass criterion:

0. Three misconceptions to fix first (how exclusivity actually works, the 20-minute micro-drama rule, AI labeling)
1. Qualifications audit — which doors your licenses open
2. Platform selection — four "species" of platforms and a default distribution order
3. Douyin Rights Center: the three account roles (individual / institution / multi-role)
4. Contract models & breach clauses
5. Rights paperwork — the four-piece evidence set for AI dramas, plus timestamps
6. Dual-version production & pre-flight check
7. Upload flow, end to end
8. Review rejections: the three common landmines, filing tiers
9. Platform-by-platform notes (Kuaishou / WeChat ecosystem / Huolong / iQIYI / Bilibili / big-screen platforms)
10. Four legitimate IP channels (incl. the WeChat official-account novel channel opened Oct 2026)
11. Monetization & settlement — revenue-share formulas, minimum guarantees, incentive tiers
12. Second-round licensing, overseas (TikTok), big-screen, anti-piracy
13. Watch item: interactive dramas
14. Expiry warnings — which rules change fastest and how often to re-check

## How to use

1. Download `SKILL.md`.
2. Put it in your agent's skills directory (or paste the full text to any AI assistant as its operating procedure).
3. Say: *"I have an AI animated drama ready to launch"* — it starts at Section 0 and walks you through, section by section.
4. Don't skip sections: a failed item stops the walk until it's fixed.

## Data currency — please read

Platform rules, incentive programs, contract models and settlement terms change **fast**. Every figure, threshold and ratio in this checklist reflects an **October 2026** check and is provided for reference only — **always re-verify against each platform's latest official rules and your actual contract before acting**. This skill is not a revenue promise or legal advice; read contracts clause by clause, and consult a professional on major decisions.
